# CLOSE SCRIPT — Day 2, minutes 60–120

The 60-minute sales close. Engineered valley → permission ask → stack reveal (11 bonuses + 1 fast action) → if-anchoring → price reveal → 3 closes layered → Deployment Promise → walk to back.

**Aligned to the final SR design** (forest-green branded one-pager). All dates, prices, and bonuses match the printed version attendees see.

**Memorize the spine** (bolded lines below). The connective tissue can flex; the spine cannot.

---

## Pre-frame (the engineered valley before this — minutes 60–66)

You've just told your vulnerable story. Future-paced them to December. They're settled, quiet, leaning forward. Music has faded. You're standing again.

**Do not rush. The value of what you're about to do is set by the silence before you do it.**

---

[Pause 3 seconds.]

[Wait. They will nod. Wait until you can see the nodding before continuing.]

# OBJECTION BANK — what to say if anyone holds you up

These are the responses to the 12 most common objections. The crew uses these too — abbreviated versions in `CREW_BRIEF.md`.

---

### O-1 · "I need to think about it."

> **"I get that. Let me ask — when someone tells me they need to think about it, in my experience it usually means one of three things. Either you're not sure this is for you. Or you don't have all the information. Or it's a money issue. Which one is it for you?"**

(Cardone's 3-bucket reframe. Forces specificity. Once you know which bucket, you address the actual concern.)

---

### O-2 · "I need to talk to my [spouse / business partner]."

> "Of course. What would you need to be able to tell them, in one sentence, that would make this a yes?"

> "And remember — the Deployment Promise exists for exactly this situation. We don't refund. We deliver until your AI is running. So even if they want guarantees, the guarantee is RESULTS, not money. That's a stronger story than most refund offers you've ever heard."

---

### O-3 · "$1,997 is a lot."

> "$1,997 over 12 months is $5.50 a day. One AI employee replaces a $1,500-a-month copywriter. One AI replaces a $4,000-a-month VA. The math works after week one."

> "And if cash flow is the friction — we have a 3-pay option. $665 today, $665 in 30 days, $665 in 60 days. That's about $22 a day for 90 days, then nothing. After that you own it."

---

### O-4 · "I can already do this with ChatGPT for $20/month."

> "You absolutely can. ChatGPT is the engine. The cohort is the chassis. ChatGPT alone is a $20-a-month tool the average user gets $40 of value from. The cohort is the system that turns that same engine into a deployed AI employee saving you 10 hours a week."

> "Honest question — if you could already build an AI employee saving you 10 hours a week with $20/month, would you be in this room?"

(They almost always laugh and concede.)

---

### O-5 · "I'm not technical."

> "Perfect. That's the entire reason this exists. The whole bootcamp is built around — and explicitly NAMED — the principle that the only skill required is clarity. Not coding. Not technical. Clarity."

> "Stand up if you saw the demo on stage today. The volunteer wasn't technical. They pressed one button. That's the bootcamp — three weekends in a row, with you."

---

### O-6 · "I'll do it myself with [free YouTube content / a course I already bought]."

> "That's a real option. Some people do. And some of those people are still trying to figure it out two years later. The cost of doing it yourself isn't $0 — it's six months of evenings, a year of false starts."

> "$1,997 buys you 12 hours of me live, 100+ AI skills already built, and a community of founders doing it WITH you. The cost-of-time math always wins for people who actually want this done."

(Cardone cost-of-inaction frame.)

### O-7 · "Will this work for my industry / country / size?"

> "Two answers. One — yes. The AI Employee blueprint isn't industry-specific. The brief / voice / output structure works for restaurants, agencies, e-commerce, real estate, doctors. Every type."

> "Two — if you specifically want to talk through your case, grab one of the team at the back of the room before you decide. We don't want anyone in the cohort who shouldn't be."

(The "talk to the team" move filters stallers — real fits actually go talk and convert.)

### O-12 · "Can I get back to you tomorrow / next week?"

(This is a soft no with a smile.)

> "I respect that. Two things — first, the room-only bonuses are gone when you walk out. After this event, even if you say yes tomorrow, you don't get Founder OS, you don't get the 10X Accelerator, and the price jumps to $2,497."

I'm not going to push you. But I will ask — what are you actually going to do differently tomorrow that you can't do right now?"

(Cardone's "rather have a no" mechanic. Let the silence sit. Either they sign up or you let them go warm.)

# Crew posture during the close

While Danny is on stage, the crew at the back is:

1. **Visible.** Standing. Smiling. Clipboards out. Order forms ready.
2. **Quiet during the silences.** Especially the 5-second hold on the price reveal. No movement.
3. **Active during "I'M IN."** The moment the room starts to stand and walk back, the crew opens up. Order forms move. QR codes visible. Payment terminal visible.
4. **Patient with stallers.** The hard close is Danny's; the team's role is helpful, not pushy. Use the objection bank. Lead with the **Deployment Promise**, not money-back (we don't have money-back).

---

# Final note to Danny

The close is choreography. The room is rehearsed (by you, over two days) to do exactly what you ask. By the time the price hits the slide, the audience has already declared three times that they're the kind of person who buys this.

The close isn't where you sell — it's where you give them permission to do what they've already decided.

If the room doesn't move, it's not because the price is wrong or the offer is bad. It's because the rehearsal didn't take. Diagnose backward: did you fire the anchor 4 times? Did you do the Eker declaration with full body? Did the live demos land? If any of those slipped, the close was always going to be soft.

So protect the 3.5 hours. The 60 minutes will take care of itself.
