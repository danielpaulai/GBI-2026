# DAY 1 — "THE AWAKENING"
## 90 minutes · GBI Singapore · NO SALE

**Goal:** Plant identity. Land one massive demo. Hook them for Day 2's last session.

**Energy curve:** Explode (dance) → Connect (songs) → Build (teach) → Peak (demo) → Settle (Day 2 hook)

**Sales beat:** None today. Leave the stack open. End with cliffhanger.

---

## The Suggestology Toolkit (use throughout)

T. Harv Eker's 10 Making the Stage techniques. Tag each in delivery. Always say "thank you" after every audience response.

| # | Technique | Example for AI content |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** | Frame in questions | *"Are questions important? Do questions wake you up?"* |
| **S2** | Repetition (audience repeats) | *"AI is the new electricity. Everyone, AI is the new..."* |
| **S3** | "Say that with me please" | *"AI CEOs are the leaders of 2030. Say that with me — 'I am an AI CEO.'"* |
| **S4** | Repeat with "what" at end | *"You can deploy your team. You can deploy your what?"* → *"TEAM!"* |
| **S5** | Quizzer — get them to repeat | *"Marketing AI saved Sarah 14 hours. How many?"* → *"14!"* |
| **S6** | Leave last word off | *"Don't hire another human. Build an AI ___"* → *"EMPLOYEE!"* |
| **S7** | Agreement question | *"Does that make sense? Everyone follow?"* |
| **S7a** | Tag-ons | *"It's beautiful, isn't it?"* |
| **S8** | Relating question | *"Can anybody else relate to that?"* |
| **S9** | Precede statement with question | *"So why does this matter? It matters because..."* |
| **S10** | "How many of you..." | *"How many of you stayed up past midnight working on stuff AI could've done?"* |

**Rule:** never say a flat statement when you can make it a question. Audience that answers questions belongs to you.

---

## Cross-cutting elements

- **Anchor word:** "Tuesday" — plant in min 25, drop every time you describe the post-cohort future
- **Catchphrase:** *"Who builds the AI?"* → *"I DO!"* — plant min 12, trigger every transition
- **Physical anchor:** open hand to chest on every "yes" moment
- **Streisand hook:** Mission: Impossible theme — 30s clip plays before every demo, every time

## BLOCK B · THE SONG EXERCISE · 4–12 min · E↑↑↑

The crowd-warming move. 6 universally-known song snippets. Audience stands, sings, energy compounds, the room becomes one tribe.

**Setup line (4:00):** `[S1]` *"How many of you know there are songs in this world that EVERYONE knows? Let me play one — when you know it, stand up and SING."*

## BLOCK D · THE AI AWAKENING — WHY NOW · 17–35 min · E= → E↑

The teaching block. Densest content. Apply Suggestology heavily.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| **17:00** | `[S9]` *"So why does AI matter NOW? Why is 2026 different from 2024?"* |
| **17:30** | **Origin story.** 4 sentences. Before / After. *"I used to work 80-hour weeks running a 1-person business. I burned out. Then I deployed AI agents. Now I do 25 hours and make 3× more. I'm not a coder. I just figured it out. So can you."* |
| **20:00** | **Quizzer block** `[S5]`: Three numbers the audience repeats. *"AI investment in 2025 hit $250 billion. How much?"* → *"$250 BILLION!"* / *"Knowledge worker hours replaced by AI in 2026: 1.2 billion. How many?"* → *"1.2 BILLION!"* / *"Average ROI from one deployed AI agent: 14×. How much?"* → *"14×!"* |
| **23:00** | `[S1]` *"Is AI a fad? Is this 1995 internet, or 2001 dotcom? Will this matter in five years?"* |
| **24:00** | `[S4]` *"AI is going to replace tasks, not WHAT?"* → *"PEOPLE!"* |
| **25:00** | **Anchor word planted**: *"Last Tuesday I deployed three AI employees in 90 minutes. The Tuesday before, I was still doing all that work myself."* |
| **26:00** | `[S7a]` *"It's a big shift, isn't it? And it's accelerating, isn't it?"* |
| **27:00** | **The 2030 Vision** (3 min) — paint two scenarios. The operator who adopted in 2026: by 2030 has $5M business with 2 humans + 12 AI agents. The operator who waited: by 2030 still doing the work themselves. |
| **30:00** | `[S3]` *"Hand on your heart. Say with me — 'I am the one who adopts.' Again. LIKE YOU MEAN IT."* |

## BLOCK G · LIVE DEMO — MARKETING AI · 60–80 min · E↑↑↑ · PEAK

The single highest-leverage 20 minutes of Day 1.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| **60:00** | `[S1]` *"Are we going to do this live? Are you ready? Is anybody nervous for me?"* |
| **60:30** | **Volunteer pull**: *"I need someone who does NOT consider themselves technical. Stand up if that's you."* Pick from BACK of room. |
| **61:30** | Mission: Impossible theme starts (low). Walk volunteer up. Hand them mic. |
| **62:00** | Warm them up: *"What's your name? What's your business?"* (60 seconds.) |
| **63:00** | **Co-create the prompt** `[S10]` *"Audience — what's the most annoying marketing task in YOUR business right now? Three shouts. Loudest cheer wins."* Take 3 shouts. Loudest goes on screen. |
| **64:00** | Set the stake: *"If this works, every person in this room can do this Tuesday morning. If it fails, I owe Jakarta an apology."* |
| **65:00** | *"[Volunteer] — when I say go, press ONE button. That's it. Your job is theatrical, not technical. GO."* |
| **65:30** | Volunteer presses. AI generates 7-day campaign in <60 seconds. |
| **66:30** | **The Pause** — 3 full seconds of silence. Look at screen, volunteer, room. |
| **66:33** | **Payoff line**: *"That's a week of work. In 45 seconds. Done."* |
| **67:00** | `[S4]` *"Tuesday morning, your AI will work? Tuesday morning, your AI will WHAT?"* → *"WORK!"* |
| **68:00** | Anchor volunteer back to seat with stagecraft (handshake, applause, optional "I built an AI on stage" gift). |
| **69:00** | `[S7]` *"Does that make sense? Did you actually believe that was real?"* |
| **70:00–80:00** | **Teach how it works.** Brief / Voice / Output structure. Plain English. Anchor word callbacks. Catchphrase trigger. |

## STATE ENGINEERING RULES

- Stand every 15-20 minutes minimum
- Power breath × 5 at every block opening
- Pattern interrupt every 12 minutes (sound, movement, callback, music)
- Read your 3-5 barometer audience members every 60 seconds — if 2 of them drift, change state in next 60 seconds

---

## THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE

**No sale today.** End with the stack open. Tomorrow does the work.

If you find yourself drifting toward pitching, redirect to the Day 2 hook. The audience needs to leave Day 1 trusting you, not deciding about you.
