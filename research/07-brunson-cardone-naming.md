# 07 — Brunson / Cardone Naming Pattern Research

**Mission.** Replace "AI CEO" with an outcome-loaded, stage-shoutable name for Danny's $1,497 GBI Singapore 2026 offer. Pattern-match Brunson, Cardone, Hormozi, Eker, Robbins + active 2025–26 AI sellers.

## Benchmarks (verbatim where found)

### Russell Brunson
| Program | Type | Outcome embedded | Stage subtitle pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One Funnel Away Challenge** | RESULT + TIME | Build one profitable funnel in 30 days | "You are only one funnel away from the life you've dreamed of." |
| **Two Comma Club** | NUMBER (visual: $1,000,000) | Hit $1M through one funnel | Walk across the stage at Funnel Hacking Live with a plaque. |
| **2CCX** | NUMBER ($10M) | Scale a single funnel to $10M | Same mechanic, scaled. |
| **DotCom / Expert / Traffic Secrets** | METHOD ("Secrets") | Hidden knowledge transfer | "The underground playbook for…" |
| **Funnel Hacker / Inner Circle** | IDENTITY | Tribe membership | "Become the funnel hacker." |

**Brunson formula:** NUMBER (with visual hook) + TIME + IDENTITY tribe. The number does the selling — "Two Comma Club" = $1M without saying $1M. That's the trick.

### Grant Cardone
| Program | Outcome | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| **10X Growth Conference / 10X Business Summit** | MULTIPLIER | "10X Your Income, Business & Life" |
| **10X Money / Cardone University** | MULTIPLIER | Same 10X anchor everywhere |
| **Closer's Survival Guide / Sell or Be Sold** | METHOD + threat | Binary outcome wording |

**Cardone formula:** SINGLE NUMBER (10X) repeated across every product. Subtitle is always a triplet — "Income, Business & Life." Shoutable. Stadium-sized. The number IS the brand.

### Alex Hormozi
| Program | Pattern | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **$100M Offers / $100M Leads / $100M Money Models** | DOLLAR + NOUN | Each book is a single lever to $100M |

**Hormozi formula:** $XM + ONE WORD. The dollar number sets the ceiling; the noun sets the lever. 5M+ copies sold on this naming alone.

### T. Harv Eker (relevant for Asia / MMI audience — same buyer)
| Program | Pattern |
|---|---|
| **Millionaire Mind Intensive** | IDENTITY + INTENSITY ("Intensive") |
| **SpeedWealth** | OUTCOME + SPEED (compound word) |
| **Quantum Leap** | MAGNITUDE metaphor |

**Eker formula:** Wealth identity + speed/intensity modifier. "Millionaire Mind" sets the identity transformation — exactly what GBI / MMI buyers walk in expecting.

### Tony Robbins
| Program | Outcome verb |
|---|---|
| **Unleash the Power Within** | Verb-led ("Unleash") |
| **Date with Destiny** | Metaphor (relationship with future self) |
| **Business Mastery** | IDENTITY + outcome |

**Robbins formula:** Verb or metaphor + emotional state shift. Less number-driven, more transformation-driven.

### Active AI Stage Offers 2025–26
| Seller | Program | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Liam Ottley** | AAA Accelerator (AI Automation Agency) | METHOD + ROLE |
| **Iman Gadzhi** | Monetise 2.0 / Make Money Online Challenge 2.0 / AI Shadow Operating | OUTCOME verb |
| **Cole Gordon** | 7-Figure Selling Academy / 30 Day Closer | NUMBER + IDENTITY / TIME + IDENTITY |
| **Sam Ovens** | Consulting Accelerator | ROLE + speed |
| Mass-market books | "AI Money Machine 2026" / "AI Cash Machine" | OUTCOME + MECHANISM (machine metaphor is everywhere right now) |

**Insight for Danny:** "AI CEO" is a ROLE name (like "Closer," "Consultant"). Roles don't sell on stage — outcomes do. Brunson, Cardone, Hormozi all moved away from role names years ago. Note: "AI Money Machine" is already commodified across $9 Amazon books — avoid it.

## The Verbatim Subtitle Pattern Danny Should Copy

**Cardone triplet (highest stage ROI):**
> **"[NAME]: [Outcome 1], [Outcome 2] & [Outcome 3]."**

Cardone's live-stage example: *"10X Your Income, Business & Life."*
Danny's plug-and-play: *"10X With AI: Your Income, Your Business & Your Life."*

**Why this beats everything else for a live Asia stage:**
1. Triplets land on stage (rule of 3, breath-paced).
2. The audience finishes the line with you on Day 2.
3. Works in any language MMI translates into.
4. Already the dominant stage pattern in the room — zero re-education.

**Avoid:** "AI Money Machine" (commoditized on Amazon), "AI Empire" (status word, not outcome), "AI CEO" (role, not result), "AI Freedom" (vague — Asian business-owner audience pays for numbers, not vibes).

## Recommendation

Rename **AI CEO Cohort → "10X WITH AI"**.
Stage tagline: **"10X Your Income, Your Business & Your Life — With AI."**
Mechanism inside the offer keeps the "AI Employee" name (it's the *what they get*; "10X With AI" is the *what it does*).
