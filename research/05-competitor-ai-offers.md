## SECTION B — 5 Program-Name Patterns (rated)

**Pattern 1: Single Aspirational Noun + "System" / "Bootcamp"** — *AI Advantage Bootcamp*, *Agency Navigator*, *Quantum Growth*, *Consulting Accelerator*. Calm, premium, repeatable. **Best for stage**: works in Mandarin translation.

**Pattern 2: "The [Power Word] Engine / Machine / Empire"** — *AI Profit Machine*, *AI Money Machine*, *ChatGPT Income Empire*, *Avatar Empire*. Loud, aspirational, used in cold-traffic ClickBank-style offers. Reads as hypey at premium rooms.

**Pattern 3: Identity Transformation** — *Prime Mover* (Brunson), *AI CEO*, *30 Day Closer*. Names what you BECOME, not what you get. Sticky. Easy to chant.

**Pattern 4: The Mechanic Has a Name** — *Clone Builder System* (AI Advantage), *Godfather Offer*, *Magic Lantern*, *MIFGE*. Ownable, memorable, defensible. Best when the mechanic itself is taught.

**Pattern 5: Possession Language** — *Your AI Employee*, *Your Business Brain*, *Your Clone*. Frames the buyer as already owning it.

## SECTION C — Copywriting Patterns That Excite Buyers

### Sentence shapes that work (no em dashes)

**1. The Outcome + Timeline + Risk Reversal triple**
> "Land your first AI client in 90 days, or your money back."
> "Reclaim 15 hours a week, guaranteed."
> "Build your AI employee live with us. If by July 1 it isn't running, full refund."

**2. The Definite-Article Asset**
Every bonus opens with "The." *The Vault. The Blueprint. The Library.* This makes a digital file feel like a thing you own.

**3. Lifetime + Forever + Yours**
> "Lifetime access to all recordings."
> "Re-attend any future cohort. Forever."
> "It's yours. Keep everything."
Buyers grade these phrases as the highest-value lines on the page.

**4. Numbers-as-credibility**
> "218+ training videos." "2,400+ members." "37 ready-to-use AI tasks." "$21K in software discounts."
Specific odd numbers > round numbers. "$15,488" beats "$15,000."

**5. Daily / Weekly cadence words**
> "Daily QA." "Weekly hot seats." "Office hours every week."
Cadence proofs aliveness. A static course feels dead. A weekly call feels like a relationship.

**6. The Plug-and-Play Frame**
> "Plug-and-play templates." "Copy-paste systems." "Ready-to-deploy blueprints."
Removes the implementation tax. Buyer hears "I don't have to figure this out."

### Power words observed across pages
build, deploy, install, unlock, reclaim, plug-and-play, copy-paste, ready-to-deploy, lifetime, forever, vault, blueprint, library, brain trust, the [thing] system, daily, live, real, deployed, running, your AI, your clone, your employee.

### Sensory phrases (no em dashes) that make a bonus feel valuable in 1-2 sentences

> "By Sunday night, your Content Engine is live, posting in your voice, on your real business."
> "Open Founder OS Monday morning. The week ahead is already planned."
> "Drop a Loom in. Get a polished SOP back in 90 seconds."
> "Walk in green. Walk out with a deployed AI employee."

The pattern: **time-of-day + concrete output + on-your-business**. Buyer can mentally rehearse the win.
