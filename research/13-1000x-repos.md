## The 3 single highest-leverage integrations (do this week)

If you do nothing else from this doc, do these three. They shift the demo's *category*, not just its polish.

### 1. `pmndrs/uikit` — volumetric HUD inside R3F
- **URL:** https://github.com/pmndrs/uikit
- **Stars:** ~3.5k | Active weekly
- **Why it's #1:** Renders shadcn-style components INSIDE R3F. Floating panels in actual 3D space, not flat DOM overlays. The difference between "web app" and "Tony Stark's lab."
- **GBI moment:** Wing roster in Act 2 floats in volumetric space, not screen-pinned. The CMO/CRO/COO/CFO panels orbit the CEO orb in real depth.
- **Build effort:** ~3 days
- **Verdict:** **SHIP IT** — single biggest perceptual upgrade per hour of work.

### 2. `paper-design/shaders` — ambient Apple Vision Pro backdrops
- **URL:** https://github.com/paper-design/shaders
- **Stars:** ~3k | Weekly 2026
- **Why it's #2:** Their warp/grain/mesh-gradient shaders are GPU-cheap and look exactly like Vision Pro spatial backdrops. Kills the SaaS-slop background problem instantly.
- **GBI moment:** The ambient layer between acts becomes cinema. Replaces static gradients behind Arwes chrome.
- **Build effort:** ~6 hours
- **Verdict:** **SHIP IT** — biggest "ambient cinema" win per hour.

### 3. MaximeHeckel's WebGPU compute particles
- **URL:** https://github.com/MaximeHeckel/blog.maximeheckel.com
- **Stars:** ~1.3k | Active 2026
- **Why it's #3:** The "10M particle attractor" sketch in Maxime's TSL articles ships as runnable demos. Your orb stops looking like every other AI orb on the internet.
- **GBI moment:** The orb pulse during Act 3 CEO synthesis. Particles attracted to the orb's center, scattered when wings fire, converged on the synthesis moment.
- **Build effort:** ~2 days to port one sketch into your TSL pipeline
- **Verdict:** **SHIP IT** — your visual signature.

## The synthesis-of-synthesis (why this matters)

Adding these 17 repos doesn't make the demo "better." It shifts the demo's **category**.

Right now the demo lives in the "very impressive AI dashboard" bucket — same bucket as every YC company's demo day, every CopilotKit example, every shadcn AI starter. Audience auto-compares against what they've already seen.

Add **uikit + paper-design/shaders + Heckel's WebGPU particles** and you cross into "what app IS this — I've never seen this aesthetic before." That's the only category that survives a 1000-person stage because it short-circuits comparison shopping in the audience's head.

Layer in **the official MCP servers + Figma MCP + Playwright MCP** and the wings stop being theater. The audience watches Gmail drafts appear in their own muscle-memory of Gmail. That's not a demo — that's their Tuesday morning.

Then the **Vercel AI Chatbot + Claude Agent SDK + Cline** triple at the close collapses the perceived gap between "Hollywood" and "buildable." Students leave with three repos they can clone before dinner.

Net effect: Danny isn't selling a $1,497 cohort. He's selling the only path the audience can see to reproduce something they couldn't categorize 90 minutes earlier.

That's what closes 8% conversion at $1,497 instead of 2%.
