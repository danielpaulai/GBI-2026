# DAY 1 RUN SHEET — 90 minutes

**Goal of Day 1:** plant identity, build belief, peak with two demos, leave them leaning forward into Day 2. **Do not close on Day 1.** End with the stack OPEN.

**Energy curve target:** wave (peak / valley / peak / valley / peak), not a flat line.

**Anchor word:** [PICK ONE — recommendation: "Tuesday."] · **Catchphrase:** [PICK ONE — recommendation: "Who builds the AI?" → "I DO!"]

**Music cues used:** Entrance Anthem (SE-19), Demo Drop = Mission: Impossible (SE-20), Reflection Bed (SE-23), Movement (SE-22).

---

## Block A · STATE FIRST · 0–7 min · E↑

**Pre-stage:** AV plays Entrance Anthem starting 30s before you walk on. Lights up. You enter walking, microphone on.

**Sequence:**
- **0:00** — Walk on. Mic check (don't apologize for the sound — own it). Music fades on cue at 0:30.
- **0:30** — *"Everyone — on your feet. Right now. STAND UP."* (SE-1, immediate state-set.)
- **0:45** — *"Hands on your knees. Power breath with me — short, sharp, in through the nose. Out the mouth. Five times."* (SE-5 / Robbins.)
- **1:30** — *"Find someone next to you. High-five. Tell them: 'I'm here to build something.'"* (SE-4 movement + commitment in 30s.)
- **2:15** — *"Sit down. Now look at me."*
- **2:30** — **Code of Honor.** *"For the next two days we agree to three things. We listen fully. We participate fully. We don't leave decisions to tomorrow. Say it back: 'I agree.'"* (Singer.)
- **3:00** — **First declaration.** *"Hand on your heart. Say with me: 'I am the kind of person who builds systems that work for me.'"* Pause. *"Again. Say it like you mean it."* (Eker mechanic. This is the one you'll fire 4 more times today.)
- **4:00** — Pick your 3–5 **barometer people** silently — front-left, front-right, mid-back, far-back. By name in your head. They are your read-the-room signal for the next 86 minutes.
- **5:00** — **Big Promise.** *"In the next 90 minutes, you're going to learn exactly how to deploy your first AI employee — without writing code, without hiring a developer, and without spending another year on the wrong thing."* (Brunson.)
- **6:00** — Hook-to-end bait. *"And at the very end of Day 2, I'm giving away [a specific bonus] only to the people in this room who are actually here for both sessions. Don't miss it."* (Brunson retention bait.)

**Slide cue:** title slide → quick logo flash → blank for the declaration → big-promise text only.

## Block B · IDENTITY POSITION · 7–17 min · E=

- **7:00** — **Origin story.** Your two-dad / before-after arc. *"I used to be the guy who… [3-sentence past]. Now I'm the guy who… [3-sentence present]."* No résumé. (Kiyosaki.)
- **10:00** — **Self-segmentation.** *"Quick show of hands. Hands up if you currently have ZERO AI employees in your business. [pause] Hands up if you have ONE. Hands up if you have THREE OR MORE. Hands up if you have TEN OR MORE."* Most hands stay down. *"Look around. The reason that hand-count looks like that is the reason you're in this room."* (Kiyosaki self-confront.)
- **12:00** — **The Anchor word planted.** Drop your chosen word for the first time, naturally, in a sentence. ("Tuesday" example: *"Last Tuesday I built three AI employees in 90 minutes. The Tuesday before that I was the guy who would have spent a week doing the same work."*) (SE-15.)
- **13:30** — **Catchphrase planted.** *"In this room, when I ask 'Who builds the AI?' you say 'I DO!' Let's try it. Who builds the AI?"* — *"I DO!"* — *"Louder. WHO BUILDS THE AI?"* — *"I DO!"* (SE-7. You'll trigger this 12+ times across two days.)
- **15:00** — **The One Thing.** *"If you remember nothing else from this whole event, remember this — [your one sentence]."* Write it on the slide. Big text. Hold it.
  - *Recommended one-sentence: "Don't hire another human. Build an AI employee instead."*

**Slide cue:** photo of you "before" / photo "after" → quadrant-style hand-count slide → blank for catchphrase plant → ONE-THING slide held for 60 seconds.

## Block C · DEMO #1 — MARKETING AI · 17–32 min · E↑↑ · PEAK 1

This is your first WOW. Full anatomy in `LIVE_DEMOS.md`.

- **17:00** — **Volunteer pull.** *"I need someone who does NOT consider themselves technical. Stand up if that's you."* Pick from the back of the room. Walk them up while Mission: Impossible theme starts (SE-20).
- **18:00** — **Co-create the prompt** (SE-9). Take 3 shouted answers from the room. Pick the loudest. Put it on screen.
- **19:00** — Volunteer presses ONE button. Camera on their face (AV preset cut).
- **19:30** — AI generates a 7-day campaign live. <60 seconds.
- **20:30** — **The Pause.** Three full seconds of silence. Look at the screen, look at the volunteer, look at the room. (SE-27.)
- **20:33** — **Payoff line.** *"That's a week of work. In 45 seconds. Done."*
- **21:00** — Anchor the volunteer back to seat with stagecraft (handshake / brief gift / applause). They become a lunch-table evangelist.
- **22:00** — Bridge: *"Watch what just happened. One person, one button, one prompt — and a week of marketing work disappeared. Now let's go deeper."*
- **23:00–32:00** — **Teach: how the Marketing AI works.** Show the structure. Three components. Don't go technical — go conceptual: brief, voice, output. Anchor word callback at min 30. Catchphrase trigger at min 32.

**Slide cue:** Volunteer cam → split-screen old way (calendar with 7 hrs blocked) vs. new way (campaign in <60s) → 3-component diagram of Marketing AI.

## Block D · BELIEF BREAK #1 — VEHICLE · 32–45 min · E= → E↑

- **32:00** — **SE-6 Turn-to-Your-Neighbor.** *"Turn to the person next to you. 30 seconds: tell them the ONE marketing task you'd hand to an AI tomorrow morning."* Pull 2 answers from the floor.
- **34:00** — **Secret 1 — "AI doesn't work for [Asia / my industry / my size]."** State the belief. Then break it with a **Singapore/Asia case study story** — someone unlikely (older, traditional industry, modest size) who built an AI employee and got a tangible result. Story arc: who they were → what they tried → what worked → where they are now.
- **40:00** — **Pattern interrupt.** *"Stand up. Both arms in the air. Hold for 10 seconds. Now drop your hands and write down the next thing I say."* (SE-2.)
- **40:30** — Write on the slide: *"AI doesn't care about your industry. It cares about your prompt."*
- **41:00–45:00** — Mini-teach: the prompt is the product. Three rules of a good AI-employee prompt. Anchor word callback. Catchphrase trigger.

**Slide cue:** TTN prompt → photo / case-study slide → written principle → 3 rules of a prompt.

## Block E · DEMO #2 — SALES AI · 45–60 min · E↑↑ · PEAK 2

- **45:00** — **Volunteer pull #2.** *"Different person this time. I need someone who has tried — and given up — on cold outreach."* Pull from a different part of the room. Mission: Impossible theme starts.
- **46:00** — **Pull a SECOND volunteer to be "the prospect."** They stay seated, but the AI's message will go to them and they'll read it out loud.
- **47:00** — **Co-create the target.** *"Whose LinkedIn URL should we send to?"* Take a shout. Put it on screen.
- **47:30** — Volunteer 1 presses the button. AI generates a personalized cold message in 20 seconds.
- **48:00** — **Volunteer 2 reads the message out loud** as if they were the prospect.
- **48:30** — **The Pause.**
- **48:33** — **The Sticky Moment.** Volunteer 2 says (you've coached them, or it lands organically): *"I'd actually book a call after that message."* Audience laughs. The room buys.
- **49:00** — Side-by-side slide: AI message vs. a generic templated message. The room sees the gap.
- **51:00** — Anchor both volunteers back with stagecraft. Catchphrase trigger.
- **52:00–60:00** — **Teach: how the Sales AI works.** Voice + brief + target. Don't go technical. Show before/after of one of YOUR own outreach examples.

**Slide cue:** Volunteer cams → AI message side-by-side with generic → before/after of your outreach.

## Block F · BELIEF BREAK #2 — INTERNAL · 60–73 min · E= → E↑

- **60:00** — **SE-3 Dance break (planned slump counter).** Cue "Don't Stop Me Now" or "Uptown Funk." 60 seconds. No rules. Lights flash. Music cuts. Sit.
- **61:00** — **Secret 2 — "I'm not technical enough."** State the belief. Then break it with the **unlikely-builder story** — older / non-coder / Asia-based / built one in 30 minutes / specific result. Make this story hit home.
- **66:00** — **SE-12 Fear naming.** *"Here's what your little voice is saying right now: 'That story sounds great but it's not me. I really am too non-technical. AI is for kids.'"* (Singer's Little Voice mechanic.) *"That voice is a liar. And I'm going to prove it on Day 2 when I put YOU on stage."* (Day 2 hook bait.)
- **68:00–73:00** — Mini-teach: the technical skill required is zero. The skill required is **clarity** — knowing what you want done. Anchor word callback. Catchphrase trigger.

**Slide cue:** dance break has lights only, no slide → photo / case-study of the unlikely builder → "the only skill is clarity" slide.

---

## Block G · BELIEF BREAK #3 — EXTERNAL · 73–82 min · E↑

- **73:00** — **Secret 3 — "Even if it worked, I have no time / no team / my industry is different."** State the belief. Break by showing the AI handling exactly that constraint — quick screen-share or prepared example, not a live demo (you've burned the demo budget for Day 1).
- **78:00** — **Second declaration moment.** *"Stand up. Hand on your heart. Say with me: 'I am the kind of person who builds systems that work for me.' Again. Louder. SAY IT LIKE YOU MEAN IT."* (Eker.) Fire your physical anchor (open hand to chest) for the second time.
- **80:00** — Sit. Quiet voice. (Engineered valley before Day 2 hook.)

**Slide cue:** screen-share / prepared example → blank for the declaration → black slide for the valley.

## Block H · DAY 2 HOOK · 82–90 min · E↓ → E↑

The deliberate valley + future-pace + cliffhanger that pulls them back tomorrow.

- **82:00** — **SE-23 Reflection bed in.** Hans Zimmer "Time" or Einaudi. Quiet voice. *"Close your eyes. It's Tuesday morning, July 7. You wake up. You walk to your laptop. Three AI employees you built in this cohort have already done their work overnight. There's a marketing campaign waiting for your approval. There are five sales messages that went out. There's a report that tells you exactly what to focus on this week. You haven't even had coffee yet."*
- **84:00** — Pause. Let them sit.
- **85:00** — *"Open your eyes. That's not a fantasy. That's three weekends from now."*
- **86:00** — **The Day 2 hook.** *"Tomorrow on Day 2, I'm going to put one of you on stage and we're going to build YOUR AI employee live. The one you've been thinking about for the last 90 minutes. And then I'm going to show you exactly how to take the same thing home tonight and have it running by Tuesday."* (Anchor word.)
- **88:00** — **Catchphrase final fire of Day 1.** *"Who builds the AI?"* — *"I DO!"* — *"Tomorrow."* — *"I DO!"*
- **89:00** — **No close.** *"Get some food. Get some sleep. Tomorrow is going to be the day. I'll see you here. Don't be late."*
- **89:30** — Music: SE-25 Exit / Tribe Track. People walk out humming.

**Slide cue:** black → "Tuesday" anchor word slide → "Day 2: 9:00 AM" final card.
