## Translation note for the reviewer

Critical phrasing decisions in this draft:

1. **"AI 员工" (AI Employee)** — used throughout. Alternative was "AI 助手" (AI assistant). "员工" (employee) is more aspirational and fits the framing better, but check that it doesn't read as too formal/corporate to the Singaporean Chinese audience.

2. **"打造一支..团队" (build a team)** in the headline — collectivist framing fits the Asia audience better than the English "build for yourself."

3. **"无价" (priceless)** for the risk reversal anchor — direct translation of the English. Common phrase in Chinese marketing.

4. **Currency** — kept as USD ($1,497, $549 × 3). Consider whether to also show SGD equivalent for clarity. Current conversion (~SGD 2,025 / SGD 745 × 3) — but this needs the day-of rate.

5. **"Founder OS"** — left in English. If the team has a Chinese product name, swap it in.

6. **"Tuesday morning" anchor language** from the English script — not present in this brief intentionally. The Chinese version focuses on outcome (24-hour AI team, 5+ hrs saved) rather than the recurring "Tuesday" comedy callback, which doesn't translate well.

7. **Tone** — drafted in semi-formal Chinese (适合 Singaporean Chinese business audience). If the actual audience skews younger / more casual, soften certain phrases (e.g., "您" → "你" for second person).
