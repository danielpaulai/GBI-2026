## The Suggestology Toolkit — Use Throughout

T. Harv Eker's 10 Making the Stage techniques. Tag each in the run sheets as `[S1]`, `[S2]`, etc. Use 1-2 per teaching block minimum. Always say "thank you" after every audience response.

| # | Technique | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | Frame in questions | When you'd normally make a statement | *"Are questions important? Do questions wake you up?"* |
| **S2** | Repetition (flipchart, repeat) | When a concept is critical | *"AI is the new electricity. Everyone, AI is the new..."* |
| **S3** | "Say that with me please" | When planting an identity | *"AI CEOs are the leaders of 2030. Say that with me — 'I am an AI CEO.'"* |
| **S4** | Repeat with "what" at end | When reinforcing a key word | *"You can deploy your team. You can deploy your what?"* → *"TEAM!"* |
| **S5** | Quizzer — get them to repeat | Stats, numbers, results | *"Marketing AI saved Sarah 14 hours. How many hours?"* → *"14!"* |
| **S6** | Leave last word off | When the answer is obvious | *"Don't hire another human. Build an AI ___"* → *"EMPLOYEE!"* |
| **S7** | Agreement question | After a strong point | *"Does that make sense? Everyone follow?"* |
| **S7a** | Tag-ons (isn't it / aren't they) | Soften statements into invitations | *"It's beautiful, isn't it? Questions are powerful, aren't they?"* |
| **S8** | Relating question | After a vulnerable moment | *"Can anybody else relate to that?"* |
| **S9** | Precede statement with question | Open every teaching block | *"So why does this matter? It matters because..."* |
| **S10** | "How many of you..." | Self-segmentation, hand-raise | *"How many of you stayed up past midnight last week working on stuff AI could've done?"* |

**Rule of thumb:** never say a flat statement when you can make it a question. Audience that answers questions belongs to you.

# DAY 1 — "THE AWAKENING" · 90 MINUTES

**Goal:** Plant identity. Land one massive demo. Hook for Day 2. NO SALE.

**Energy curve:** Explode (dance) → connect (songs) → build (teach) → peak (demo) → settle (Day 2 hook)

## BLOCK A · OPENING DANCE — "GASOLINE" · 0–4 min · E↑↑↑

**Music cue:** "Gasoline" (the version you've chosen) — 90 seconds, fade out at 1:30.

- **0:00** — Music drops. You walk on dancing. Mic on.
- **0:15** — *"EVERYONE — ON YOUR FEET. RIGHT NOW. We're about to start with energy because that's how this room is going to feel for the next two days."*
- **0:30** — *"Move with me. Move your body. If you don't move, you don't learn."* — Dance for 60 seconds.
- **1:30** — Music fades. *"SIT. Or stand. Whatever. Look at me."*
- **2:00** — `[S10]` *"How many of you woke up tired this morning? How many of you walked in here unsure if this is going to be worth your time?"* (Hands go up.)
- **2:30** — `[S1]` *"Did you know I'm going to ask you a lot of questions in the next two days? Are questions important? What do questions do? Is it possible for me to teach you only in questions? Let's find out."*
- **3:30** — `[S3]` *"Hand on your heart. Say with me: 'I'm here to learn.' Again. SAY IT LIKE YOU MEAN IT."*

## BLOCK B · THE SONG EXERCISE · 4–12 min · E↑↑↑

The crowd-warming move. 6 universally-known song snippets, ~30 seconds each. They stand, they sing, energy compounds, the room becomes one tribe.

**Setup line (4:00):** `[S1]` *"How many of you know there are songs in this world that EVERYONE knows? What's a song that, no matter where you're from, you can sing? Let me play one — when you know it, stand up and SING."*

### The 6-Song Set (memorize the order — energy builds)

| # | Song | Snippet | Why it works | Stage move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sweet Caroline** (Neil Diamond) | "Sweet Caroline / BAH BAH BAH / Good times never seemed so good" | The "BAH BAH BAH" is universally known, easy entry | Point mic at audience on the BAH BAH BAH |
| 2 | **Dancing Queen** (ABBA) | "You can dance / you can jive / having the time of your LIFE" | Asia knows ABBA cold | Dance with arms up |
| 3 | **We Will Rock You** (Queen) | The stomp-stomp-clap section | Built-in audience participation | Get them clapping the rhythm |
| 4 | **Don't Stop Believin'** (Journey) | "Just a small town girl / livin' in a lonely world" | Universal anthem | Hold mic out, let them sing |
| 5 | **September** (Earth Wind & Fire) | "Do you remember / the 21st night of September" | Joyful, danceable | Dance, clap |
| 6 | **Bohemian Rhapsody — Galileo section** (Queen) | "GALILEO! Galileo! Galileo!" call-and-response | Massive payoff, high energy finish | Conduct the audience like a choir |

**Total time:** 8 min including transitions. Each song 60-75 seconds with quick setup line.

**Setup line for each:** `[S1]` + `[S10]` — *"How many of you know this one? Who recognizes the first 5 seconds?"* → play → they sing.

**End the exercise (11:30):** *"SIT. Catch your breath. Look around — that is your tribe for the next 48 hours."*

**The plant (11:50):** `[S2]` Plant the catchphrase. *"In this room, when I ask 'Who builds the AI?' you say 'I DO!' Try it. WHO BUILDS THE AI?"* → *"I DO!"* → *"Louder!"* → *"I DO!"*

## BLOCK D · THE AI AWAKENING — WHY NOW · 17–35 min · E= → E↑

This is the teaching block. Densest content. Apply Suggestology heavily.

- **17:00** — `[S9]` *"So why does AI matter NOW? Why is 2026 different from 2024?"*
- **17:30** — Drop your origin story. 4 sentences. Before / after.
  - Example: *"I used to work 80-hour weeks running a 1-person business. I burned out. Then I deployed AI agents. Now I do 25 hours and make 3× more. I'm not a coder. I just figured it out. So can you."*
- **20:00** — **Quizzer block** (S5): Three numbers the audience repeats.
  - *"AI investment in 2025 hit $250 billion. How much?"* → *"$250 BILLION!"*
  - *"Knowledge worker hours replaced by AI in 2026: 1.2 billion. How many?"* → *"1.2 BILLION!"*
  - *"Average ROI from one deployed AI agent: 14×. How much?"* → *"14×!"*
- **23:00** — `[S1]` *"Is AI a fad? Is this 1995 internet, or is this 2001 dotcom? Will this matter in five years?"* (Frame the binary, let the room decide.)
- **24:00** — `[S4]` *"AI is going to replace tasks, not WHAT?"* → *"PEOPLE!"*
- **25:00** — **Anchor word planted**: *"Last Tuesday I deployed three AI employees in 90 minutes. The Tuesday before, I was still doing all that work myself."*
- **26:00** — `[S7a]` Tag-on: *"It's a big shift, isn't it? And it's accelerating, isn't it?"*
- **27:00** — **The 2030 Vision** (3 min) — paint two scenarios:
  - The operator who adopted in 2026: by 2030 has a $5M business with 2 humans + 12 AI agents
  - The operator who waited: by 2030 still doing the work themselves while their competitor's AI eats their market
- **30:00** — `[S3]` *"Hand on your heart. Say with me — 'I am the one who adopts.' Again. LIKE YOU MEAN IT."*
- **31:00** — `[S8]` Relating question: *"How many of you have felt overwhelmed by how fast AI moves? How many secretly worry you're already behind?"* (Hands.)
- **32:00** — *"Good. You're not behind. You're early. Half of this room hasn't deployed a single AI yet. And by the end of Day 2, the ones who decide to do something will be ahead of 99% of business owners on the planet."*
- **33:00** — `[S6]` Leave-last-word-off: *"You can build a business that runs by ___"* → *"ITSELF!"*
- **34:00** — Catchphrase trigger: *"Who builds the AI?"* → *"I DO!"*

## BLOCK F · WHAT THIS MEANS FOR BUSINESS OWNERS · 50–60 min · E= → E↑

The "this is for YOU" block. Identity bridge.

- **50:00** — `[S9]` *"So what does this mean for you, the business owner? Three things."*
- **50:30** — **Three reframes** (3 min each):
  1. **Hire vs Deploy** — hiring takes 6 weeks + $5K. Deploying takes 6 minutes + $20.
  2. **Bottleneck shifts from labor to imagination** — the question is no longer "can I afford to do this?" but "did I think to do this?"
  3. **Solo founder economics inverted** — what used to require a team of 8 is now possible with you + AI executives
- **57:00** — `[S4]` *"Can you build a 7-figure business as a solo founder? You can build a 7-figure business as a WHAT?"* → *"SOLO FOUNDER!"*
- **58:00** — `[S8]` *"Has anyone here actually been the bottleneck in their own business? Like — you're the one stopping growth? Anyone? Hand up if that's you."*
- **59:00** — *"Good. Honest hands. That ends today."*

## BLOCK G · THE LIVE DEMO — MARKETING AI · 60–80 min · E↑↑↑ · PEAK

The single highest-leverage 20 minutes of Day 1.

- **60:00** — `[S1]` *"Are we going to do this live? Are you ready? Is anybody nervous for me?"*
- **60:30** — **Volunteer pull**: *"I need someone who does NOT consider themselves technical. Stand up if that's you."* Pick from BACK of room.
- **61:30** — Mission: Impossible theme starts (low). Walk volunteer up. Hand them mic.
- **62:00** — Warm them up: *"What's your name? What's your business?"* (60 seconds.)
- **63:00** — **Co-create the prompt** `[S10]` *"Audience — what's the most annoying marketing task in YOUR business right now? Three shouts. Loudest cheer wins."* Take 3 shouts. Loudest goes on screen.
- **64:00** — Set the stake: *"If this works, every person in this room can do this Tuesday morning. If it fails, I owe Jakarta an apology."*
- **65:00** — *"[Volunteer] — when I say go, you press ONE button. That's it. Your job is theatrical, not technical. GO."*
- **65:30** — Volunteer presses. AI generates 7-day campaign in <60 seconds.
- **66:30** — **The Pause** — 3 full seconds of silence. Look at screen, volunteer, room.
- **66:33** — **Payoff line**: *"That's a week of work. In 45 seconds. Done."*
- **67:00** — `[S4]` Anchor with "what": *"Tuesday morning, your AI will work? Tuesday morning, your AI will WHAT?"* → *"WORK!"*
- **68:00** — Anchor volunteer back to seat with stagecraft (handshake, applause, small "I built an AI on stage" gift).
- **69:00** — `[S7]` *"Does that make sense? Did you actually believe that was real?"* (Yes, because it was.)
- **70:00–80:00** — **Teach how it works.** Brief / Voice / Output structure for marketing. Plain English. Anchor word callbacks. Catchphrase trigger.

## BLOCK H · ENGINEERED VALLEY + DAY 2 HOOK · 80–90 min · E↓ → E↑

The deliberate slowdown that makes Day 2 inevitable.

- **80:30** — **Future pace**: *"Close your eyes. It's Tuesday morning, July 14. You wake up. Walk to your laptop. Three AI employees you built have already worked all weekend. Marketing campaign ready. 12 sales messages went out overnight. Report tells you exactly what to focus on. You haven't even had coffee."*
- **83:00** — Pause. Don't fill it.
- **84:00** — *"Open your eyes. That's not a fantasy. That's three weekends from now."*
- **85:00** — **Day 2 hook (the bait)**: *"Tomorrow on Day 2, I'm putting one of YOU on this stage. We're building YOUR AI employee live. Then I'm showing you exactly how to take it home tonight and have it running by Tuesday."*
- **87:00** — **Catchphrase final fire**: *"Who builds the AI?"* → *"I DO!"* → *"Tomorrow."* → *"I DO!"*
- **89:00** — *"Get food. Get sleep. Tomorrow's the day. Don't be late."*
- **89:30** — Music: SE-25 Exit / Tribe track ("September" by Earth Wind & Fire — they leave humming what they sang earlier).

# THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE

If the audience is silent for more than 90 seconds, you've broken the Suggestology contract. Fix it within 30 seconds: either ask a question, trigger a declaration, or change physical state.

Audience that responds belongs to you. Audience that watches passively belongs to whoever sells next.
