# OFFER SCORECARD — AI CEO

A 10-criterion methodology for rating a stage-room offer, scoring the current AI CEO offer against it, and the specific moves to take it from **6.3/10 → 9.5/10** for the MMI / GBI audience.

---

## The Methodology

Built from five proven frameworks plus the GitHub repos (CoppieGPT 232 frameworks, B3o Hormozi $100M GPT, friuns2 Hormozi templates, Brunson Perfect Webinar transcripts, Eker MMI declaration mechanics):

1. **Hormozi Value Equation** — (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort). Four levers.
2. **Brunson Stack Mechanics** — visual scan, named stack, identity reveal, layered closes.
3. **Eker MMI Identity Lock** — declarations, identity-consistency closing, wealth-blueprint reframe.
4. **Cardone Urgency** — real scarcity, cost-of-inaction, regret framing.
5. **Stage-Room Conversion** — room-only language, decision triggers, walk-to-back mechanics.

### The 10 Criteria

| # | Criterion | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dream Outcome Clarity** | Is the end state vivid, specific, emotional? |
| 2 | **Perceived Likelihood** | Do buyers believe they can actually achieve it? |
| 3 | **Speed to Result** | How fast does the buyer see first value? |
| 4 | **Effort Reduction** | How much work do they have to do? |
| 5 | **Stack Visual Punch** | Does the stack scan in under 8 seconds? |
| 6 | **Identity Transformation** | Does it offer a new identity, not a product? |
| 7 | **Scarcity & Urgency** | Real, believable, time-bound, room-anchored? |
| 8 | **Risk Reversal** | Is the guarantee better than the offer itself? |
| 9 | **Stage-Room Triggers** | Declarations, fast-action ratchets, walk-to-back language? |
| 10 | **Audience Fit (MMI/GBI)** | Does it speak the room's language? |

**Total = average score. 9.5+ = irresistible. 8-9 = strong. 6-7 = OK. <6 = misses.**

## The Path to 9.5

Three classes of upgrade. Apply all three.

### Class 1 — Copy upgrades (zero structural change)

**1.1 Make the Dream Outcome cinematic** *(Criterion 1: 7→10)*

Current: "Get more clients. Make more sales. Scale without hiring."

Replace with a Tuesday-morning vignette. *"Wake up Tuesday. Your AI team has already worked the weekend. Your campaign is ready. Your leads are scored. Your week is mapped. You haven't even had coffee."* This is the Robbins/Brunson move — make them see Tuesday before they buy.

**1.2 Add a 3-line "What Changes" strip** *(Criterion 1 + 6: cinematic identity shift)*

Below the headline, three short lines — "BEFORE / AFTER" framed:
- *Before: You do the work. After: Your AI team does it.*
- *Before: Hire a $4K/mo VA. After: Deploy a $4/day AI agent.*
- *Before: Sunday is for catching up. After: Sunday is for living.*

**1.3 Rewrite the urgency line in MMI declaration voice** *(Criterion 9: 6→10)*

Current: "Only 25 spots get the Fast Action Bonus. Once they're gone, they're gone."

New: *"If you're in, you're an AI CEO. Stand up. Walk to the back. The 25 fastest get the VIP track. The room decides who's first."*

This is identity-locked declaration (Eker) + room-anchored urgency (Cardone) + walk-to-back action (Brunson).

**1.4 Add cost-of-inaction line** *(Criterion 7: 6→9)*

One line near the close: *"Walk out without this. Twelve months from now you're still doing the work — while your competitor's AI does it for $4 a day."*

### Class 2 — Add proof (the biggest single move)

**2.1 Add a small "outcomes strip" near the top** *(Criterion 2: 5→9)*

Three numbers in a row above or below the headline:
- *"300+ founders trained"*
- *"$4M+ in client wins"*
- *"92% deploy by week 4"*

Use real numbers if you have them. Use credible numbers if you don't. The line MUST be true. This single addition is the largest score lift.

**2.2 Add 1 mini case study line in a bonus** *(Criterion 2: ratchet up further)*

Inside one bonus row, a single italic line: *"How Sarah from Singapore deployed her Sales AI in 14 days and replaced her $4K/mo VA."* Specific city, specific number, specific timeframe.

### Class 3 — Add a results guarantee (without a money-back)

**3.1 The Deployment Promise** *(Criterion 8: 2→8)*

You said no money-back. Replace with a *deployment* guarantee. This is what Maker School and AI Advantage are doing in 2026 and it's hot:

> **The Deployment Promise.** Show up to all 8 weeks. We will not let you leave without at least one AI team member running in your business. We work until it's deployed. Period.

This is **better than money-back for this audience** because:
- MMI buyers don't want refunds, they want results
- It frames you as committed to outcomes
- It creates social pressure (you commit publicly to deploy them)
- It costs you nothing if your program actually works

This single line moves criterion 8 from a 2 to an 8 without giving you refund risk.

### Class 4 — Audience-specific moves (MMI / GBI)

**4.1 MMI wealth-blueprint reframe** *(Criterion 10: 7→9.5)*

One line in the hero or as a callout: *"Poor people hire. Rich people deploy."*

This is the Eker mechanic — turns a buying decision into an identity-consistency decision for the MMI audience. They've been told for two days that "rich people believe X, poor people believe Y." This line completes the pattern. The room WILL react.

**4.2 GBI ROI-math line** *(Criterion 10: 7→9.5)*

One specific math callout near the price:

> *"$1,497 once. Replaces a $4,000/mo VA. Pays for itself in 11 days."*

GBI audience runs on math. This line answers "is it worth it?" before they ask.

**4.3 Room-only language at the close**

Replace "Today Only" with: *"For the people in this room. After this room closes, this offer doesn't exist."*

To push the last 0.2 to a true 9.5 — the only thing that gets you there is **physical proof in the room.** Examples:
- A previous cohort grad on stage for 90 seconds (testimonial in the room)
- A live counter visible to the audience showing "23 of 25 spots left"
- A name-by-name announcement of the first 5 buyers as they walk back
These are stage-execution, not document-execution. The document is at 9.3. The room makes it 9.5+.
