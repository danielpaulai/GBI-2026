# DAY 2 RUN SHEET — 120 minutes

**Goal of Day 2:** open hot, peak twice, engineer one valley, land the close on a peak. The lethal slot is min 50–80 — that's where you've been losing them. Solution: PEAK at 35–55 with the most ambitious demo, then put the audience to WORK co-creating from 55–70 so they're active when fatigue would normally hit.

**Anchor word + catchphrase:** same as Day 1. By Day 2 they own them. Trigger more aggressively.

**Energy curve:** wave / wave / engineered valley / peak close.

**Music cues:** Entrance Anthem (SE-19), Mission: Impossible (SE-20), Stack Reveal (SE-21 — Hans Zimmer "Time" or "Heart of Courage"), Decision Moment (SE-24 — "In the Hall of the Mountain King"), Reflection Bed (SE-23), Exit/Tribe (SE-25).

---

## Block A · ENERGY SNAP-BACK · 0–10 min · E↑

- **0:00** — Entrance Anthem in. Walk on. Mic on.
- **0:30** — *"Yesterday I told you AI doesn't replace you. AI replaces…"* — audience: *"THE WORK YOU HATE!"* — *"Louder. AI replaces…"* — *"THE WORK YOU HATE!"* (SE-17 catchphrase, instant re-anchor of yesterday's state.)
- **1:00** — *"On your feet. Power breath × 5. Hand on your heart. Say with me — 'I am the kind of person who builds systems that work for me.'"* (Third declaration of the talk. Locked in.)
- **3:00** — **Roll-call recognition.** *"Hands up if you went home last night and tried to build something with AI. [pause] Hands up if you DIDN'T but you thought about it. [pause] Hands up if you came back hungry. [pause] Look around. THIS is your tribe."* (SE-11.)
- **5:00** — **Re-state The One Thing.** *"Yesterday I told you the one sentence. If you remember nothing else: don't hire another human. Build an AI employee instead."*
- **6:00** — **The promise of Day 2.** *"Today is different. Yesterday I taught. Today we BUILD. By the end of these two hours, one of you will be on stage. The rest of you will have your AI employee designed on paper. Some of you will leave with one running. And then we're going to have one important conversation."* (Pre-frame the close without pre-framing it.)
- **8:00** — Catchphrase trigger #2. *"Who builds the AI?"* — *"I DO!"*

**Slide cue:** "Day 2" title → recognition slide (live count of hands) → "TODAY WE BUILD" title.

## Block B · TEACHING FRAME 4 — THE BLUEPRINT · 10–32 min · E= 

Most teach-heavy block. You earned it because Day 2 opens hot. Use the show layer aggressively to keep it alive.

- **10:00** — **The AI Employee Blueprint.** One slide. Three components. Brief / Voice / Output. Teach 4 minutes per component. (12 min teach.)
- **22:00** — **SE-6 Turn-to-Your-Neighbor.** *"30 seconds. Tell your neighbor — what's the brief, voice, and output for the FIRST AI employee you'd build? GO."*
- **24:00** — Pull 3 answers from the floor. Read them back. Connect to the Blueprint slide.
- **27:00** — **Anchor word callback** + comedy beat. Drop the word. Audience reacts. Move.
- **28:00** — **Mini-case study.** One operator's brief / voice / output → result. 4 minutes. Hard cut. (You'll be tempted to keep going. Don't.)
- **32:00** — **SE-3 Dance break.** *"On your feet. 60 seconds. Move."* "Don't Stop Me Now." Lights. Music cuts. Sit.

**Slide cue:** Blueprint diagram → TTN prompt → 3 audience answers slide → case study before/after.

## Block C · DEMO #3 — OPS AI · 33–55 min · E↑↑ · PEAK 1 — THE SHOWSTOPPER

This is the most ambitious demo and the most likely to fail. Backup recording queued one keystroke away. Full choreography in `LIVE_DEMOS.md`.

- **33:00** — **The setup.** *"Yesterday I built marketing and sales. Today we build the OS — the Operations AI. The one that pulls everything together and tells you, every Tuesday morning, what to actually focus on."* (Anchor word.)
- **34:00** — **Volunteer pull — THREE volunteers.** *"I need three people. One who runs sales. One who runs marketing. One who runs ops. Stand up — yes, you, you, and you."* (Pull from different parts of the room.)
- **35:00** — Mission: Impossible theme in.
- **35:30** — **The team setup.** Each volunteer goes to a different "tool" station on stage (or a different button on the same screen — your choice based on tech setup). They each press one button.
- **36:30** — **The Press.** All three press in sequence. AV cuts to the live dashboard.
- **37:00** — AI ties it together. Live report appears. Three things to focus on this week.
- **38:00** — **The Pause.** SE-27. Three seconds of silence. Look at the screen, the volunteers, the room.
- **38:03** — **Payoff line.** *"That report would cost you $400 a month from a contractor. It just cost you 12 cents. And it took 18 seconds."*
- **39:00** — **Anchor moment.** *"This is the difference between owning a business and being owned by one. Tuesday morning, this report is in your inbox before you have coffee."* SE-23 reflection bed kicks in for 30 seconds. Quiet.
- **40:00** — Anchor the three volunteers back to seat. Group applause. Stagecraft.
- **41:00–55:00** — **Teach: the Ops AI architecture.** Three integrations + one decision layer. Don't go technical. Show the conceptual map. Anchor word at 48. Catchphrase trigger at 53.

**Slide cue:** "OPS AI" title → 3-station setup diagram → live dashboard cut → reflection slide → architecture diagram.

## Block D · CO-CREATE — AUDIENCE BUILDS · 55–70 min · E↑ · ACTIVE PARTICIPATION

This is the antidote to the min-50–80 lethal slump. Don't teach here. Make them work.

- **55:00** — **Hand out the worksheet** (single page: Brief / Voice / Output / Trigger / Output destination). Pre-staged at every seat. *"Pull out the worksheet under your chair."*
- **56:00** — **Instructions.** *"You have 10 minutes. Pair up. Design ONE AI employee. Brief: what does it do. Voice: who is it for. Output: what does it produce. Trigger: when does it run. Destination: where does the output go. Pair up. GO."*
- **57:00** — Music in (low-energy underscore — focus music). You walk the room. Help 2–3 pairs. Be visible.
- **66:00** — **Music cut.** *"Hands up — who has a complete worksheet?"* Most hands. *"Stand up if you have a complete worksheet."* They stand. *"Look around. You just designed something most people in your industry will never have. Hand on your heart — say with me — 'I am the kind of person who builds systems that work for me.'"* (Fourth declaration. Anchor fires for the third time.)
- **68:00** — Pull ONE pair to share their AI employee out loud. 90 seconds. (SE-14.)
- **69:30** — *"Hold onto that worksheet. By the time we're done today, that AI employee is real."* (Pre-frame.)

**Slide cue:** worksheet on screen → focus underscore → audience-share slide.

## Block E · RESULTS / CASE STUDIES · 70–88 min · E↑ · PEAK 2

The "social proof" block — show the receipts. The room is now invested (they have a designed AI employee on paper). Now you prove the path is real.

- **70:00** — **3 fast case studies.** 5 minutes each. Same arc each: who they were → what they built → what changed. Pictures of real people. Real numbers. Real time-saved.
  - Case 1: Solo operator → built Marketing AI → saved 14 hrs/week.
  - Case 2: Small agency → built Sales AI → 3× outbound volume.
  - Case 3: Established business → built Ops AI → fired 1.5 contractors.
- **85:00** — **SE-13 Public declaration.** *"Stand up if you commit to deploying ONE AI employee in your business by Tuesday, June 30 — regardless of what you decide today. Stand up."* (Eker. The "regardless of what you decide today" is critical — it's a low-stakes commitment that primes the high-stakes one in 15 minutes.)
- **87:00** — Catchphrase trigger #5 of Day 2. *"Who builds the AI?"* — *"I DO!"* (Loud. Tribal.)

**Slide cue:** 3 case study slides → declaration prompt slide → catchphrase blank.

---

## Block F · ENGINEERED VALLEY · 88–98 min · E↓

You cannot land the close on a flat line. Engineer the valley. This is where the close gets earned.

- **88:00** — Sit on the edge of the stage. Mic close. Voice quiet. SE-23 reflection bed in.
- **89:00** — **Personal vulnerable story.** Not "my origin story" again. A specific moment of fear or near-failure. 4 minutes. Slow. Quiet. Don't sell anything in this story. Just let them in.
- **93:00** — Pause. Don't fill it.
- **94:00** — **Future pace** (Robbins). Quiet voice. *"Close your eyes. It's December 31, this year. You're sitting at your desk. You're looking at the year. The year you finally stopped being the bottleneck. Three AI employees you built run your business while you actually live. What's that worth to you?"* Hold for 30 seconds.
- **96:00** — *"Open your eyes."* SE-23 fades.
- **97:00** — Stand. Mic up. Voice firmer.

**Slide cue:** black slide for vulnerable story → black for future pace → blank for stand-up cut.

---

## Block G · STACK REVEAL · 98–113 min · E↑ · PEAK CLOSE

Full script in `CLOSE_SCRIPT.md`. This is the abbreviated minute-map for the run sheet.

- **98:00** — **Permission ask** (Brunson). *"Let me ask you a question. Is it okay if I spend a few minutes on something I built specifically for the people in this room?"* Wait for the room to nod. (They will.)
- **99:00** — **The pre-frame.** *"What I'm about to show you is the path. The cohort. The thing that takes you from where you are right now to your AI employees running by July."*
- **100:00** — **Stack slide build begins.** SE-21 stack music in (Hans Zimmer "Time" or "Heart of Courage"). Each component drops on a beat:
  - Component 1: The 4-week cohort itself ($4,997 anchor).
  - Component 2: Founder OS access ($2,997 anchor).
  - Component 3: 20 pre-built AI templates ($4,000+ anchor).
  - Component 4: Replays + lifetime cohort access ($2,997 anchor).
  - Component 5: Private cohort community ($497 anchor).
  - Component 6: Risk reversal ("priceless").
- **107:00** — **Total real-world value.** *"That's a real-world value of $15,488."*
- **108:00** — **If-anchoring** (5 ifs). *"If all this did was let you fire one $4K/month VA, would it be worth it? If all this did was give you back 10 hours a week, would it be worth it? If all this did was let you sleep on Sundays again, would it be worth it? If all this did was prove to you that you CAN build something — would it be worth it? If all this did was…"*
- **110:00** — **Price reveal pattern.** *"I'm not going to charge you $4,997. I'm not going to charge you $2,997. For the people in this room, today only — $1,497."* SE-21 music peaks on the number.
- **111:00** — **Hold the number on slide.** Don't talk for 5 full seconds.
- **112:00** — **3 closes layered:**
  - **Emotional (50%):** *"This is who you are now. The person who builds. Not the person who waits."*
  - **Logical (30%):** *"$1,497 over 12 months is $4 a day. One AI employee replaces work worth thousands a month."*
  - **Urgency (20%):** *"Pre-bootcamp starts June 12. That's 27 days. Founder OS access is room-only — gone when you walk out. After this room, the price is $2,497 with no Founder OS."*

**Slide cue:** stack slide builds line by line → total value reveal → price reveal slide (HOLD) → 3-close text slides.

## Block H · CTA + RISK REVERSAL + CALLBACK · 113–120 min · E↑↑↑ · PEAK FINAL

- **113:00** — **Just-One-Thing close** (Brunson). *"All I'm asking is one thing — try it for the first weekend. Show up to the pre-bootcamp on June 12. If by the end of June 13 you don't think this is the most valuable two days of your year, get every dollar back AND keep everything I've given you."*
- **114:00** — **Risk reversal hardened.** *"Show up to all 3 weekends. Build your AI employee live. If by July 1 you don't have at least one AI employee running and saving you 5+ hours a week, full refund and you keep Founder OS, the templates, the replays. Everything."* (Hormozi-grade — better than the offer.)
- **115:00** — **SE-18 Callback** to Day 1. *"Remember Day 1, hour 1, when I said your competitor in Jakarta is already 6 months ahead? That's no longer a fear. As of right now, that's no longer you."* (Closes the loop they didn't know was open.)
- **116:00** — **Code of Honor callback.** *"You agreed at the start of yesterday — we don't leave decisions to tomorrow. The decision is right now."* (Singer.)
- **117:00** — **Final declaration.** *"Stand up. Hand on your heart. Say with me — 'I'M IN.' Everyone who is in — walk to the back of the room right now. Order forms are there. Crew is there. Founder OS access starts tonight."*
- **118:00** — SE-24 Decision Moment music in. Music swells. **Don't talk over the close.** Let the room move.
- **119:00** — Watch the room. Smile. Stay on stage. People will look at you for permission to stand. Nod. Catch eyes with your barometer people.
- **119:30** — One last catchphrase. Quiet, almost reverent. *"Who builds the AI?"* — *"I DO!"*
- **120:00** — Walk off. SE-25 Exit / Tribe track in. Mac is up next.

**Slide cue:** just-one-thing slide → risk reversal slide → callback to Jakarta line → "I'M IN" slide → order form QR code + back-of-room location.
