## THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE

By the time $1,997 hits the slide, the audience has declared **three times out loud** that they're the kind of person who builds systems that work for them. Saying no becomes inconsistent with who they've publicly been for two days.

The close is choreography. The room is rehearsed (by you, over two days) to do exactly what you ask. The close isn't where you sell — it's where you give them permission to do what they've already decided.
