# GBI SINGAPORE 2026 — 3-DAY PRESENTATION MASTER DESIGN

## Executive summary

| | Slot | Length | Goal | Sales |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | First content slot | 75 min | Plant identity. One huge wow. Hook for Day 2. | NONE — leave the stack open |
| **Day 2** | Last session | 90 min | Full pitch, stack, 3 closes, walk-to-back | **THE CLOSE — $1,997** |
| **Day 3** | Breakout | 60 min | Convert fence-sitters. Deepen for buyers. Day 4 hook. | Soft re-pitch + decision deadline |
| **Day 4** | Mega/repeat | 60 min | Mac bundle. Repeat for non-buyers. | Bundle math, separate plan |

**Audience:** ~445 people, ~356 English-speaking, ~89 Mandarin (China). Mixed MMI/GBI veterans.
**Last year:** 40 sales / 335 people = 11.94% at $1,597.
**Target this year:** 60-90 sales / 445 = 13-20% conversion @ $1,997 = **$120K-$180K**.

## The strategy: why 3 days beats a single-shot pitch

A 1-day pitch forces the room to decide before they trust you. A 3-day arc lets you:

1. **Day 1** — pure value, zero sales pressure. They like you. They tell their friends at lunch about your demo. The room *primes itself*.
2. **Day 2 last slot** — by the time you're up, they've sat through Brunson-style pitches from 4-5 other speakers. You're the one they came for. The close lands soft because the trust is already there.
3. **Day 3 breakout** — fence-sitters from Day 2 (everyone who said "let me think about it") get a second touch. Buyers get deepening. Both groups leave warm for Day 4.
4. **Day 4 repeat** — Mac bundles. Anyone who walked Day 2/3 gets a bigger frame. Last shot.

This is the Brunson stack mechanism applied across 4 days instead of 90 minutes.

## Cross-cutting elements (all 3 days)

### The Streisand Hook — Mission: Impossible theme
30-second clip plays before EVERY demo, every time, identical. By Day 3, the room cheers at the first four notes. They leave humming it. They hum it Tuesday morning when they sit down to build their first AI. **That's the trigger that makes them buy.**

### The Anchor Word — "Tuesday"
Plant in Day 1, minute 12, naturally in a sentence. *"Last Tuesday I built three AI employees in 90 minutes."* Drop it every time you describe the post-cohort future. By Day 3, just saying "Tuesday" lands a moment.

### The Catchphrase — "Who builds the AI?" → "I DO!"
Plant in Day 1, minute 14. Trigger every transition. 12+ times across 3 days. By Day 3 it's tribal — the room shouts before you ask.

### The Physical Anchor
Open hand to chest. Fire on every "yes" moment. By Day 2's close, the gesture alone moves the room. Eker mechanic.

### The Wealth Reframe (MMI hook)
*"Poor people hire. Rich people deploy."* Fire on Day 1 minute 20, Day 2 minute 5, Day 2 close, Day 3 open. The MMI veterans in the room (Eker conditioning) react. The line plants the offer's worldview.

### State engineering rules
- Stand every 15-20 minutes minimum
- Power breath × 5 at every block opening
- Pattern interrupt every 12 minutes (sound, movement, callback, music)
- Pick 3-5 barometer audience members in first 5 min of Day 1 — read by them

### Tech contingency
- Mission: Impossible queued for every demo
- Backup screen recording of every demo, ONE keystroke away
- Cellular hotspot on standby (WiFi failover)
- Failure recovery card on lectern (script for: WiFi dies, volunteer freezes, AI generates weird output, joke flops, you forget your place, full stack down, heckler)

## DAY 1 — 75-MINUTE CONTENT SLOT
### "The Reveal"

**Goal:** Plant identity (you're the AI CEO guy). Land one massive wow demo. Hook them for Day 2's last session.

**NO SALE today.** End with the stack open. Leave them leaning forward.

**Energy curve:** climb / peak / settle / climb / cliffhanger

### Pre-stage (60 min before)
- 2 min power breath
- Power pose × 60s
- Recite opening 60 sec out loud
- Entrance anthem on earbud (Lose Yourself / Power / Eye of the Tiger — ONE song, never change it)
- Final ritual line: *"I'm here for them."*

### Block A · OPEN HOT · 0-7 min · E↑
**Music:** Entrance anthem in. Walk on. Mic on.

- 0:00 — Walk on. Music fades at 0:30.
- 0:30 — *"Everyone — on your feet. STAND UP."* (SE-1)
- 0:45 — Power breath × 5 (SE-5)
- 1:30 — *"Find someone next to you. High-five. Tell them: 'I'm here to build something.'"* (SE-4)
- 2:30 — **Code of Honor** (Singer): *"Three things for the next three days. Listen fully. Participate fully. Don't leave decisions to tomorrow. Say it back: 'I AGREE.'"*
- 3:00 — **First declaration** (Eker): *"Hand on your heart. 'I am the kind of person who builds systems that work for me.' Again. SAY IT LIKE YOU MEAN IT."*
- 4:00 — Pick your 3-5 barometer people silently
- 5:00 — **Big Promise**: *"In the next 75 minutes, you're going to learn exactly how to deploy your first AI employee — without writing code, without hiring a developer, and without spending another year on the wrong thing."*
- 6:00 — **Hook-to-end bait**: *"At the very end of Day 2's last session, I'm giving away [specific bonus] only to the people in this room who are actually here for it. Don't miss it."*

### Block B · IDENTITY POSITION · 7-17 min · E=
- 7:00 — **Origin story** (your 2-sentence before / 2-sentence after): *"I used to be the guy who… now I'm the guy who…"*
- 10:00 — **Self-segmentation**: *"Hands up if you have ZERO AI employees. ONE. THREE+. TEN+."* Most stay down. *"That's why you're in this room."*

## DAY 2 — 90-MINUTE LAST SESSION
### "The Close"

**Goal:** Full pitch. Stack reveal. 3 closes layered. Walk-to-back CTA. This is the money slot — design the entire 3 days backward from this 90 minutes.

**Energy curve:** open hot → peak demo → engineered valley → peak close

### Block A · ENERGY SNAP-BACK · 0-10 min · E↑

- 0:30 — **Catchphrase reactivate**: *"Yesterday I told you AI doesn't replace you. AI replaces…"* — *"THE WORK YOU HATE!"* (instant re-anchor of yesterday's state)
